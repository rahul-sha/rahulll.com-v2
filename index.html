Who am I?

Hello there. Welcome to my portfolio. Here you will find everything about who I am and my work so far as an engineer.

To begin with, hi, I am Rahul, a Manufacturing Engineer with Criswell Singapore. Before that, I was a mechanical design engineer for more than 3 years with a Singapore-based startup, where I designed, built, and fabricated 3 working HVAC prototypes. Alongside that, I gained key insights into the daily running of a startup with feedback-driven development processes.

I have multidisciplinary skills in industrial design, product development, and design for manufacturing and assembly. I begrudgingly admit I am now a specialist-generalist, with honed skills in mechanical design and prototyping.

Portfolio

Over the years, I have found myself continuously learning new things, adapting, and getting better at them. I started as an aerospace engineer with skills in aircraft design, computational fluid dynamics, and flow visualisation. Things rarely pan out the way you think, and I now find myself fully versed in a different domain of engineering, where:

  1. Manufacturing, not just designI fully appreciate the actual manufacturing process. Working with GD&T, manufacturing nuances, and more has emboldened the designer in me.
  2. Requirements matterGathering design requirements is just as important as the design itself.
  3. DfM and DfAI understand the full picture of hardware product development, with a key focus on design for manufacturing and assembly. Otherwise, the cost in hours and money is too high.
  4. Production realitiesSourcing parts, managing timelines, tracking how parts are made, and forming partnerships with OEMs and small vendors ultimately make or break a product.

There is more I have learned and contributed to, but this non-exhaustive selection should give the right idea. Pick a category to narrow it down, and open any project to see every image and video.
