Resume

Engineer, designer,
maker.

An aerospace engineering graduate of Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, and the Technical University of Munich (joint degree), I have been in Singapore for over 5 years. During my graduate studies I was fascinated by airflow and the physics of aircraft, and I dreamed of a career in aviation with companies like Airbus or Boeing. As my master's came to an end, I joined a startup in the HVAC industry, which meant it was about more than just me. I was originally tasked with the research and development of experimental compressors. With time, I found my way into the industrial design of products.
